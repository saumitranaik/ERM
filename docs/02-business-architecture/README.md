# 02 — Business Architecture

## Purpose

Bridges vision to system design: what the business does, for whom, and what capabilities it
needs — independent of any technology choice.

## What belongs here

- Business capability map (e.g., Risk Assessment, Control Testing, Regulatory Reporting as
  first-class capabilities, each traceable to a §10–15 domain)
- Value streams (e.g., "Identify risk → Assess → Treat → Monitor → Report")
- Stakeholder and persona models (Risk Owner, Compliance Officer, Internal Auditor, Board
  Risk Committee, Regulator, CISO, Business Continuity Manager, etc.)
- Organizational context (three lines of defense model, committee structures) as it
  constrains system roles/permissions

# Narration Script — Demonstration Video 02: Risk Treatment Plan Approval

Master voice-over script. Every cue is numbered, chapter-grouped, and timed. Timing method:
professional-narrator pace of **150 words/minute (2.5 words/second)**, the same standard
estimation rate `video-production-framework.md` §4 fixes for the whole series and Video 01
already used. Running timestamps are cumulative and assume cues play back-to-back with no gap;
`timeline.md` adds the short chapter-card pauses between chapters on top of this track, exactly
as it did for Video 01. Grounding for every factual claim is listed in `source-workflow.md` and
is not repeated inline here.

**Tone direction**: experienced enterprise product trainer — warm, unhurried, confident. Not a
list-reader (framework §4). This video is the **second** entry in the RISK module family, so
Chapters 2 and 3 are deliberately shortened reminders rather than the full-length treatment
Video 01 gave them (framework §13, "Domain/Module Overview... shortened after Video 01 for
later videos in the same module family"). Chapter 7 (Governance Review) keeps its required
explanatory beat — framework §13 still requires it once per video — but is written more
concisely than Video 01's own centerpiece treatment, since this audience is assumed to have
already seen the fuller explanation there (catalogue §5 dependency matrix: L1-02 "builds on"
L1-01 for risk-register-basics familiarity); see `observations.md` for this deliberate
production choice. Total narration runtime (cue timestamps below, chapter cards excluded):
**6:12**. With chapter cards and the closing-card pause added (`timeline.md`), total video
runtime is **6:47** — see `timeline.md` for the authoritative, reconciled timing. Both totals
fall inside the framework's own L1 (Simple) 6–10 minute band (§4) and, unlike Video 01's
16:55, do not exceed it — exactly the outcome the manifest's own §26 prediction anticipated for
a second video in an already-introduced module family.

Voice casting note: no synthetic or recorded voice was generated for this package (see
`asset-inventory.md`), consistent with the standing quality-bar decision (framework §14, §17).
This script is the recording brief for whoever performs it.

## Chapter 5 — Proposing a Treatment Plan

*(Screens: `03-new-treatment-plan-form.png`, `04-treatment-plan-proposed.png`.)*

**[02:15–02:18]** *(chapter card: "Proposing a Treatment Plan" / "Accept · Mitigate · Transfer · Avoid")*

**Cue 5.1 [02:18–02:40]**

> Arjun opens a new Treatment Plan. He selects a strategy — Mitigate, one of four options the
> platform supports alongside Accept, Transfer, and Avoid — names it Enhanced issuer credit

> Arjun submits it.

## Chapter 6 — Submitting for Approval

*(Screens: `05-submit-justification-dialog.png`, `06-treatment-plan-awaiting-checker.png`.)*

**[03:00–03:03]** *(chapter card: "Submitting for Approval" / "Proposed → Awaiting Checker")*

**Cue 6.1 [03:03–03:23]**

> Arjun clicks Submit for Approval. Exactly as with a risk re-assessment, this is a governed
> action — a justification is mandatory. He explains why: the existing control hasn't been
> tested against a rapid multi-notch downgrade, and this plan targets closing that gap without
> waiting for the next scheduled review.

**Cue 6.2 [03:23–03:41]**

> He submits. The status stays Proposed, but a new badge appears: Awaiting checker. And exactly
> like Demonstration 01, GOV-07 takes over — one pending action per record — and every action
> button Arjun had a moment ago is gone. He cannot approve his own plan.
